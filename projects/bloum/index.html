bloum

A slow, cosy, Sims-like world in the browser.

bloum is a shared neighbourhood you can drop into from a browser. People log in and live an ordinary life alongside characters who have personalities, needs, memories and relationships of their own. It's grounded in everyday life rather than an abstract block world, and the feel is unhurried: somewhere you can leave running and come back to.
