bloum

A slow, cosy, Sims-like world in the browser.

bloum is a shared neighbourhood you can drop into from a browser. People log in and live an ordinary life alongside NPCs who have personalities, needs, memories and relationships of their own. It's grounded in everyday life, like the original Sims, rather than an abstract block world. The feel I'm after is unhurried: something you can leave running and come back to.

The NPCs don't use a language model to decide what to do. They work like The Sims: the objects around them advertise what they offer (a fridge for hunger, a bed for rest), and each NPC weighs those offers against its needs, personality and what it remembers, then picks one of the best few. That keeps decisions instant and cheap enough to run a whole neighbourhood on one small server.

It's built with Elixir and Phoenix on the server, and a Three.js view in the browser with a fixed isometric camera and low-poly models. The whole thing runs on a $5 virtual server. This is a restart of an attempt from early 2026 that got the simulation working but never became a game.

Right now the foundations are in place and the first lot is being built: one furnished home with a handful of NPCs going about their day. Next comes playing as your own character.
